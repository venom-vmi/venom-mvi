# What if a single virus were at the root of different cancers?

**Project VENOM · Itinerant Virus Model (MVI)**

Would major corporations be willing to investigate a hypothesis outside the usual lines of research? What if the idea put forward in this dossier were true? What experiment could help us find out?

The dossier sets out how the hypothesis could be tested. The workbook traces how it was developed. The project has yet to generate experimental results of its own.
